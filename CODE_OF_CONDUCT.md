# Code of Conduct

Yept welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, abilities and levels of experience. Help make the project a place where people can ask questions, disagree about an implementation and contribute without being targeted personally.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful and specific. Discuss the work, explain disagreements with evidence, accept corrections and make room for people who are learning. Respect other people's time and privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory or sexualised abuse, threats, personal attacks, repeated unwanted contact and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable. Do not use project spaces to intimidate or exclude people.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces, and when someone represents Yept in public. It also covers private conduct related to participation in the project.

## Report a concern

Contact the maintainer, Selva Subramaniam, privately at [selva_subramaniam@outlook.com](mailto:selva_subramaniam@outlook.com). Include what happened, where and when, and relevant evidence. Share only the personal information needed to investigate. Use [SECURITY](SECURITY.md) for software vulnerabilities.

Reports will be assessed fairly and handled as privately as practical. Information may be shared with people needed to investigate or act on a report. Retaliation against a reporter or someone helping an investigation is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may ask someone to correct their behaviour, issue a warning, remove content or restrict participation temporarily or permanently, depending on severity and repeated conduct. Where practical, the person affected will receive an explanation and may ask for reconsideration through the same private contact. Serious threats or sustained harassment can lead to immediate removal.
